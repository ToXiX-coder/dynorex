Template 3 · Three Tiers of Noncustomers

The people nobody serves yet

Instead of fighting for the few people who already use a build-log app, Dynorex goes after the much bigger groups that don't keep a record at all, and the businesses that need one.

Tier 1 · Soon to be

Owners on the edge of the market

  • Keep a folder of receipts, a spreadsheet or a forum build thread
  • Type their mod list into the listing by hand when they sell
  • Tried a build-log app, but buyers still had to take their word for it

What pulls them in: the same record, but the shop confirms the work and it goes to the buyer with the car.

Tier 2 · Refusing

People who chose not to bother

  • Buyers who "never buy modified" because they can't check what was done
  • Owners who think nobody believes an owner's log anyway
  • Owners afraid that logging an off-road part gets them in trouble
  • Dealers and instant-offer sites that just discount anything "modified"

What pulls them in: a visible proof level on every entry, edits that can't hide, a VIN check, and honest emissions status the owner controls.

Tier 3 · Unexplored

Markets nobody thought of as customers

  • Tuning and install shops that want their name on good work
  • Enthusiast auctions like Bring a Trailer, where sellers write build sheets by hand
  • Specialty insurers like Hagerty, who ask owners for photos and receipts

What pulls them in: a free one-tap confirm link for shops, a build sheet ready to paste into a listing, and an insurance mod list.

Template 4 · Eliminate-Reduce-Raise-Create Grid

How Dynorex breaks away

The four actions that move Dynorex off the industry's curve and onto its own.

Eliminate what the industry takes for granted

  • Title and accident data feedsDynorex is not a vehicle history report; it records the build.
  • "Verified" and "certified" badgesEvery entry says exactly who stands behind it instead.
  • Paying to keep your own recordsLogging is free.

Reduce well below the industry

  • Effort to document a modOne tap from "Next up" to the camera; the receipt files itself.
  • Trust in the seller's word aloneEach entry shows its proof level: owner-entered, paperwork, part identified, shop-confirmed.
  • Cost to the ownerFree to log; one planned fee when the car sells.

Raise well above the industry

  • Proof behind every entryReceipts, part numbers and dyno sheets tied to the mod.
  • Honesty about changesEdits keep the old version; removals stay visible.
  • The buyer's confidence at the saleA buyer's link with the whole record and a VIN match.

Create what the industry never offered

  • Shop confirmationThe shop that did the work confirms it through a one-time link, no account.
  • A VIN check that never shows the VINThe buyer types it from the car and sees match or no match.
  • Emissions status on every engine, exhaust and electronics modWith the CARB EO number when there is one.
  • A record that moves with the carA one-time transfer code hands it to the buyer.

Everything listed is built and works in the demo, except the per-sale fee. The app isn't launched yet.
