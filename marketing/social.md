# Social plan

Platforms: TikTok and Instagram (Reels and Stories). That's where car content lives. Post from a Dynorex brand
account that a parent or guardian helps set up. Never post someone's plate, VIN or face without permission.

## Content pillars

1. **The problem (30%):** what goes wrong when you buy or sell a modified car without proof.
2. **How-to (25%):** documenting a build, what receipts to keep, what to ask a seller.
3. **Building Dynorex (25%):** a 14-year-old building a car app, what he learned this week. Only if the family is
   fine sharing that.
4. **Real builds (15%):** with permission, walk through a real car's record.
5. **Product (5%):** what's new, how to join the waitlist.

## Hooks to use

- "What would you ask before buying a car with 20 mods?"
- "I tried to find out what had been done to a used WRX. Here's what I found." (Only if it really happened.)
- "How to document your build so it's worth more when you sell."
- "The 4 kinds of proof behind a mod, from weakest to strongest."

## Video scripts (30 seconds)

**1. Buyer's nightmare**
- 0 to 3s, text on screen: "Seller says the turbo was done by a shop."
- 3 to 10s: "Okay. Which shop? When? Can you prove it?" Show a messy pile of receipts.
- 10 to 25s: screen recording of the Build Record: the entry, "Confirmed by [shop] on [date]."
- 25 to 30s: "Your build, on record. Early access link in bio."

**2. The proof ladder**
- 0 to 3s: "Not all mod lists are equal."
- 3 to 25s: walk through owner-entered, paperwork on file, part identified, shop-confirmed, with the app's
  ladder on screen.
- 25 to 30s: "Which one would you trust? Link in bio."

**3. Building it**
- 0 to 3s: "I'm 14 and I built a car app. Here's the feature I'm proudest of."
- 3 to 25s: the VIN check. Type a wrong VIN, "no match." The right one, "match." "And the buyer never sees the
  VIN."
- 25 to 30s: "Would you use this when you sell? Tell me why or why not."

Captions: 3 to 5 words a line, two lines max. Use demo data only on screen until real users agree to be shown.

**4. Dead battery? (e-moto)**
- 0 to 3s: "You're buying a used Sur-Ron. How do you know the pack isn't cooked?"
- 3 to 12s: "The seller says it's fine, and that's all you get."
- 12 to 25s: screen recording of the e-moto Build Record: a battery check with cycles and full-charge volts, and
  the controller mod showing 12 kW next to stock 8 kW. Demo data on screen, labelled.
- 25 to 30s: "Bring a meter anyway. But ask for the record. Link in bio."

**5. Is it stolen? (e-moto)**
- 0 to 3s: "Buying an e-moto off Marketplace? Check this first."
- 3 to 20s: type a frame number into the buyer's check, "match." Then tap the Bike Index search.
- 20 to 30s: "Not finding it there doesn't prove it's clean. But it's 10 seconds. Link in bio."

E-moto rules: show bikes off-road or parked, riders with helmets and gear, never street riding on an off-road
bike, never a rider's face or a frame number without permission.

## Every day (30 minutes)

- Reply to every comment.
- Comment helpfully on 5 to 10 posts from car accounts, shops and builders. No links in comments.
- Write down questions people ask. They're your next posts.

## Measure

Weekly: the top 3 and bottom 3 posts, comments (count more than likes), and waitlist signups from bio link
clicks. Record what worked in the Learnings table.
