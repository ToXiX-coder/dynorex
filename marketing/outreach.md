# Outreach drafts

Rules: one ask per message, a yes or no question, no attachments or multiple links on the first touch. Send from
your own account. A parent or guardian should know who you're contacting.

## 1. Local tuning and install shops

Goal: learn whether shops will confirm work, and get 5 to say yes to trying it.

**Subject:** quick shop question

Hi [name],

I'm Andy, and I'm building an app called Dynorex for people who modify their cars. When someone sells a modified
car, buyers usually just have to trust the seller about what was done and who did it.

In Dynorex, the owner logs a mod and sends the shop that installed it a one-time link. The shop taps confirm, no
account needed, and buyers see "Confirmed by [your shop]" on the car's record.

Would your shop be willing to confirm work that way?

Thanks,
Andy

**Follow-up 1 (4 days later):** Hi [name], one more detail that might matter: shops are never asked to confirm
anything emissions-related that isn't street legal, and you only see the job, never the owner's personal info.
Would that work for you?

**Follow-up 2 (1 week later):** Hi [name], if it's easier, I can stop by for five minutes and show you on my phone.
Would [day] work?

**Last one (2 weeks later):** Hi [name], I won't keep emailing. If it's ever useful, the offer stands. Thanks for
reading.

**In person (better than email for local shops):** "Hi, I'm Andy. I'm building an app where car owners log their
mods, and the shop that did the work can confirm it with one tap. Can I show you in 30 seconds and ask if you'd
use it?"

**Replies:**
- Yes: thank them, ask for the best email or phone for the confirm link, and add them to the beta list.
- No: ask one question, "What would make it worth it?" Write the answer in the Learnings table.
- "Tell me more": offer the 30-second demo on your phone.

## 2. People selling a modified car

Where: forum classifieds, Facebook Marketplace and groups, model subreddits, and auction listings. Only contact
people through each site's own messaging and follow its rules. Don't message anyone who says no solicitations.

**DM:**

Hi, saw your [car] is for sale. The build looks great. I'm building a free app that turns a mod list and receipts
into a record buyers can check, with the shop confirming the work. Would you want to try it for this sale? I'd set
it up with you.

**Follow-up (3 days later):** No worries if not. If you do try it, I'd love to hear if buyers asked about it.

**After the sale (the price test):** the beta is free and stays free for them. This is how the deck's
"[__] sellers who say they'd pay [$__]" gets a real number. Ask in this order, and write the answers down word for
word:

1. Did any buyer open the buyer's link or ask about the record?
2. Did it change anything: the price, how fast it sold, fewer questions?
3. If this had cost [$__] for this sale, would you have paid it? (Pick one number before you start, somewhere in
   the $30 to $100 range from the research, and use the same number with everyone.)
4. What's the most you'd have paid?

A "yes" to question 3 is a signal, not a sale. The real test comes when payments exist and a seller pays.
