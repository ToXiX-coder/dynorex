Questions

Is this a vehicle history report?
No. It doesn't cover accidents or titles. It covers what's been done to the car, and who says so.
Can't people fake receipts?
They can, which is why a receipt is only one step. Buyers see which mods the shop confirmed, and every edit.
What does it cost?
Logging your build is free.
When can I use it?
