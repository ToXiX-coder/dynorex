# Community guidelines (draft, plain language — for owner and counsel review)

Dynorex is for people who love cars. Keep it that way.

1. **Be real.** Post your own builds and photos. Don't impersonate people, shops or brands. (Report reason: Impersonation)
2. **Be decent.** No harassment, threats, hate or pile-ons. Roast a build, not a person. (Harassment, Hate, Violence)
3. **Keep it legal and safe.** No street racing promotion, evading police, or unsafe mods presented as safe. Spec numbers are owner estimates, not certifications. (Unsafe driving)
4. **No spam or scams.** No mass follows, fake giveaways, phishing links or selling outside the marketplace rules. (Spam, Scam)
5. **Keep it clean.** No sexual content. (Sexual content)
6. **Respect privacy.** Don't post other people's plates, VINs, addresses or locations without consent.

What happens: reports go to moderators, who can hide content or accounts. Every action is logged. The person
reported is never told who reported them. Block and mute work instantly and quietly.
