Processors: Supabase (database, auth, storage, functions), Expo (push delivery, builds), Sentry (crashes),
RevenueCat (purchase validation), Apple and Google (stores, IAP), NHTSA (vehicle make/model lookup; the VIN goes
to NHTSA's public decoder only when the owner taps "Fill in from VIN", straight from the phone, D-68), Mapbox
(route maps; meeting-place search when Paddock is on: the typed search and, only if the host shares location, a
point rounded to about 1 km), Anthropic (when receipt scan is on, D-68: the receipt photo the owner chooses to
scan, re-encoded on the phone without location data; read once and not stored by Dynorex; Torq chat when on,
D-48).

Not done: selling data, advertising, cross-app tracking, precise location, contact upload.
User rights in the app: export everything (Settings › Your data), delete account (with re-authentication),
edit or hide any build, turn off Nearby and push at any time.
