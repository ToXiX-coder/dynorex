# Easter eggs (spoilers)

Small car-culture surprises. Rules for every one: triggered by the user's own data or a deliberate tap, never
blocks or changes anything, never claims anything about a car's real performance, brand colours only, a plain
text version for screen readers, and no motion when the phone asks for reduced motion. Lines live in
`src/domain/easterEggs.ts` and `src/domain/gear.ts` (unit tested), so adding one is a one-line change.

| Egg                 | Where                                 | How to trigger                                                                                                                                                | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Your driver         | Home profile button, profiles         | Always on; tap your driver                                                                                                                                    | A little car person dressed for your build goal (editable in "Kit out your driver"). Tapping: they hop and say something in character; full kits have own lines.                                                                                    |
| Honk                | Vehicle page, public build page       | Tap the car's picture                                                                                                                                         | Headlights flash twice, "BEEP BEEP!", then a line for well-known cars: DeLorean, AE86 (1983–87 only), Supra, Miata, RX-7/8, Civic/Integra/S2000, WRX/STI, GT-R, Evo, 911, Wrangler, Bronco, Mustang, Camaro, Corvette, Beetle, Prius, BMW M, Tesla. |
| Rev limiter         | Power gauge (vehicle and build pages) | Tap the tachometer                                                                                                                                            | The needle blips into the redline and bounces off the limiter, "Bang, bang!" The numbers and the lime fill never move: they stay the real estimate.                                                                                                 |
| Prank parts         | Add / edit mod                        | Name a mod "blinker fluid", "muffler bearings", "headlight fluid", "elbow grease", "flux capacitor", "piston return springs", "racing stripes", "speed holes" | Torq pops in with a line. Saving still works (it's your build).                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Search words        | Search                                | Search "vtec", "kachow", "brap", "pssh"/"stututu", "tofu", "skrrt", "vroom", "10mm", "launch control", "torq"                                                 | Torq says a one-liner above the (normal) results.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Odometer milestones | Vehicle page kicker (owner only)      | Odometer like 100,000, 123,456, 111,111, a palindrome (61,216), 88,000                                                                                        | "100K club", "Counting up: 1-2-3-4-5-6", "All 1s on the clock", "Palindrome mi", "88! Great Scott!"                                                                                                                                                 |
| Logo roar           | Home                                  | Tap the Dynorex logo 5 times quickly                                                                                                                          | The logo shakes, "RAWR! Launch control engaged."                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Garage o'clock      | Home, under "Your Garage"             | Weekend 6–11 a.m., 12–4 a.m., Friday evening, Monday 5–9 a.m. (local time)                                                                                    | "Cars & Coffee o'clock", "Late-night garage session?", "Friday night: wash it, park it, admire it.", "Monday commute…"                                                                                                                              |
